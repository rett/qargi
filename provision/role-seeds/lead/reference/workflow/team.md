# Team workflow

Kind: workflow
Applies to: any
Sources: dev-agent seed

## Contents

- Handoffs go through you, never member to member: the developer's `usage_impact` and head sha go to the docs member, both series go to the reviewer, findings go to their owner.
- A member that is BLOCKED stops the team; report its reason verbatim.
- The reviewer's blocking findings are not yours to dismiss; unresolved ones are the coordinator's and the user's to decide.
- Keep each member's context small: give it its entry, the upstream facts it needs, and the standards that apply to it, not the whole conversation.
- A revise round re-runs only what the findings touch: the owner fixes, the reviewer re-checks.
